Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Rhythms is a browser rhythm game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play, sign in with Google, customize a profile, or appear on the leaderboard.

What the game uses

  • Google sign-in: Firebase Authentication handles sign-in. It provides an account identifier and may process your Google display name, email address, and profile photo. Rhythms uses the account identifier to keep your data with your account and uses your display name and photo for your profile.
  • Profile and leaderboard: Your chosen nickname, bio, profile picture, and best scores are stored in Firebase Firestore. These profile and leaderboard records can be read publicly and shown to other players. Do not put private information in your bio.
  • Progress and settings: The game keeps settings, scores, cosmetics, gold, and rhythm maker work in your browser's local storage. When you use cloud saving while signed in, a copy of your game save is stored in Firestore for that account. Cloud saves are restricted to that account by database rules.

How it is used and shared

This information runs the game, restores your progress, and displays profiles and leaderboard rankings. The game has no advertising or analytics integration and does not sell your information. Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage; GitHub Pages hosts the website. These services may process technical data under their own policies.

Your choices

You can play without signing in, edit your nickname, bio, and picture in Profile, or export a local game save. Signing out does not delete cloud data. Browser storage can be cleared in your browser settings. Cloud profiles and saves remain until they are removed.

Questions and removal requests

Contact the maintainer through the Rhythms GitHub Issues page for privacy questions or to request removal of account data. Issues are public, so do not post your email, account identifier, or other private details there. The maintainer can arrange a private way to verify account ownership.
